PID Monitor Trainer

Practice the MiniRAE 3000 key presses, screens and settings without the monitor. Unofficial training aid. It does not measure gas.

PID TRAINER

Vapor at the inlet

Pick a vapor and the monitor responds to it the way a 10.6 eV lamp would. The monitor's own settings do not change.

One-finger shortcuts

You can also hold the real keys: MODE alone for power, MODE and N/− together for Programming Mode.

What the inlet is sampling

Key guide
Power on
Hold MODE until the display starts.
Power off
From the reading screen hold MODE for 3 seconds, then keep holding through the 5-second countdown.
Icons
Battery at the top right. On the line under the reading: the pump (it turns while the pump runs and flashes a warning if stalled) and the datalog disk while logging. With Pump Duty Cycle below 100% and a reading under 2 ppm, the pump icon alternates with a pause mark. With Radio Power on (Monitor Setup, Radio Power), an antenna and five signal bars appear at the top right beside the battery.
Flashlight key
Press the round key to switch the flashlight on. Press again to switch it off.
Timeout
In Programming Mode, 60 seconds with no key press returns to the reading screen without saving the change in progress.
Search mode
Set under Monitor Setup, Op Mode. Sampling starts and stops on request, the second screen shows Avg and Peak in place of TWA and STEL, and an alarm beeps 1 to 7 times a second, faster at higher concentration.
User mode
Basic shows the reading, TWA/STEL/Peak, date and time, gas summary and PC Comm screens. Advanced adds "Change reference?" and "Change M. Gas?" and skips the password.
Alarms
HIGH, OVR and pump stall: 3 beeps a second. LOW: 2. STEL, TWA, CAL (calibration needed) and NEG (reading below the stored zero): 1. Clear only resets the Peak value.
N/−
Steps to the next screen or menu item, moves to the next digit, answers No.
Reverse
Hold N/− for 5 seconds on a screen with a scroll arrow. The arrow flips and N/− then steps backward. Hold 5 seconds again to flip it back.
Y/+
Selects, raises a digit, answers Yes. On the reading screen it tests the alarm, clears a latched alarm, or restarts a stalled pump.
MODE
Backs out, finishes an edit, exits Programming Mode.
Programming
From the reading screen hold MODE and N/− for 3 seconds. Password is 0000, so press MODE (Enter) to confirm. A wrong password opens Calibration only. Advanced user mode skips the password.
Range
Reads 0 to 999.9 ppm in 0.1 ppm steps and 1,000 to 15,000 ppm in 1 ppm steps. Above 15,000 ppm the reading holds at the top of the range and an OVR alarm sounds. The vapor sample slider goes to 16,000 ppm so you can trigger it.
Gas library
222 pre-programmed compounds in the monitor's alphabetical order. Gases with no 10.6 eV correction factor are listed but cannot be selected.
Vapor
Pick a vapor under "Vapor at the inlet" and the slider becomes that vapor's true ppm. The monitor shows what its lamp would report: the true ppm divided by the vapor's correction factor while set to Isobutylene, and the true ppm itself once Meas. Gas is set to the same vapor. Span gas is always 100 ppm isobutylene.
Time multiplier
Speeds up the exposure clock so TWA, STEL, Avg and the datalog count build faster. STEL shows dashes until 15 minutes of exposure time have passed. Readings, countdowns and key holds stay in real time.
Calibrate
Pick the gas under "What the inlet is sampling" first, then run Zero Calib or Span Calib. Bump checks the response against the span gas without changing the calibration. After you change a setting the soft keys switch to Save and Undo: Y/+ saves, N/− discards. Span Calib first asks "Change?": Yes edits the span value, No moves on to applying gas. Countdowns run three times faster than the real 30 seconds.

Screens and menus follow the published user guide and a recorded walkthrough of the monitor. Small details can differ with firmware version. The monitor's gas library and the look-up drop-down both use RAE Systems Technical Note TN-186 (MiniRAE 3000 pre-programmed compounds), 10.6 eV column.

Critical error

This change would make the monitor unusable

Undo reverses only this change. Reset returns to the reading screen with every setting at its default.
