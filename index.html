Air Monitor Trainer

Pick a vapor and the monitor responds to it the way a 10.6 eV lamp would. The monitor's own settings do not change.

One-finger shortcuts

You can also hold the real keys: MODE alone for power, MODE and N/− together for Programming Mode.

What the inlet is sampling

Key guide
Power on
Hold MODE until the display starts. The buzzer gives one long tone while the logo screen shows.
Power off
From the reading screen hold MODE for 3 seconds, then keep holding through the 5-second countdown. The buzzer and the red lights pulse together once a second while it counts.
Icons
Battery at the top right. On the line under the reading: the pump (it turns while the pump runs and flashes a warning if stalled) and the datalog disk while logging. With Pump Duty Cycle below 100% and a reading under 2 ppm, the pump icon alternates with a pause mark. With Radio Power on (Monitor Setup, Radio Power), an antenna and five signal bars appear at the top right beside the battery.
Flashlight key
Press the round key to switch the flashlight on. Press again to switch it off.
Timeout
In Programming Mode, 60 seconds with no key press returns to the reading screen without saving the change in progress.
Search mode
Set under Monitor Setup, Op Mode. Sampling starts and stops on request, the second screen shows Avg and Peak in place of TWA and STEL, and an alarm beeps 1 to 7 times a second, faster at higher concentration.
User mode
Basic shows the reading, TWA/STEL/Peak, date and time, gas summary and PC Comm screens. Advanced adds "Change reference?" and "Change M. Gas?" and skips the password.
Alarms
HIGH, OVR and pump stall: 3 beeps a second. LOW: 2. STEL, TWA, CAL (calibration needed) and NEG (reading below the stored zero): 1. Clear only resets the Peak value.
N/−
Steps to the next screen or menu item, moves to the next digit, answers No.
Reverse
Hold N/− for 5 seconds on a screen with a scroll arrow. The arrow flips and N/− then steps backward. Hold 5 seconds again to flip it back.
Y/+
Selects, raises a digit, answers Yes. On the reading screen it tests the alarm, clears a latched alarm, or restarts a stalled pump.
MODE
Backs out, finishes an edit, exits Programming Mode.
Programming
From the reading screen hold MODE and N/− for 3 seconds. Password is 0000, so press MODE (Enter) to confirm. A wrong password opens Calibration only. Advanced user mode skips the password.
Range
Reads 0 to 999.9 ppm in 0.1 ppm steps and 1,000 to 15,000 ppm in 1 ppm steps. Above 15,000 ppm the reading holds at the top of the range and an OVR alarm sounds. The vapor sample slider goes to 16,000 ppm so you can trigger it.
Gas library
222 pre-programmed compounds in the monitor's alphabetical order. Gases with no 10.6 eV correction factor are listed but cannot be selected.
Vapor
Pick a vapor under "Vapor at the inlet" and the slider becomes that vapor's true ppm. The monitor shows what its lamp would report: the true ppm divided by the vapor's correction factor while set to Isobutylene, and the true ppm itself once Meas. Gas is set to the same vapor. Span gas is always 100 ppm isobutylene.
Time multiplier
Speeds up the startup sequence, calibration countdowns, how fast readings rise and fall, and the exposure clock, so the monitor boots and calibrates sooner, readings settle sooner, and TWA, STEL, Avg and the datalog count build faster. STEL shows dashes until 15 minutes of exposure time have passed. Key holds stay in real time. At 1× zero and span calibration count down their real 30 seconds. At 1× startup takes its real time, about 8 seconds.
Calibrate
Pick the gas under "What the inlet is sampling" first, then run Zero Calib or Span Calib. Bump checks the response against the span gas without changing the calibration; if the right gas or setting is missing for a zero, span or bump, the trainer pauses it and tells you what to press or change. After you change a setting the soft keys switch to Save and Undo: Y/+ saves, N/− discards. Span Calib first asks "Change?": Yes edits the span value, No moves on to applying gas. Countdowns run three times faster than the real 30 seconds.

Screens and menus follow the published user guide and a recorded walkthrough of the monitor. Small details can differ with firmware version. The monitor's gas library and the look-up drop-down both use RAE Systems Technical Note TN-186 (MiniRAE 3000 pre-programmed compounds), 10.6 eV column.

MULTI-GAS TRAINER

Air Monitor Trainer

One-finger shortcuts

Gas at the inlet

Key guide
Power on
Hold MODE until the display starts. The buzzer gives one long tone while the logo screen shows. With Fast Startup on, the model, firmware and sensor list take about 18 seconds. With it off, four screens for each sensor and the settings screens follow, about 90 seconds in all. Zero At Start is on by default: a "Sensor Warmup" screen runs for about 15 seconds, then press Start for the 60-second fresh air calibration. After that the reading screen shows the normal baseline values. Raise the time multiplier to shorten the wait.
Power off
From the reading screen hold MODE and keep holding through the 5-second countdown. The buzzer and the red lights pulse together once a second while it counts.
N/−
Hygiene mode steps through Peak, Min, STEL, TWA, date and time, temperature, battery, runtime, VOC gas status, LEL gas status, and "Enter Communications Mode?", which leads to a choice of PC, AutoRAE 2 or Exit. Search mode shows Average in place of STEL and TWA.
Y/+
On the reading screen: tests the alarms, clears a latched alarm, or restarts a stalled pump. On Peak or Min it asks "Clear Peak And Minimum Values?".
Programming
Hold MODE and N/− for 3 seconds. Basic user mode asks for the password the first time after power-on: the four digits start as stars, so press Y/+ once on each to make it 0, N/− to move right, then MODE for Done. Advanced skips it. Menus: Calibration, Measurement, Alarms, Datalog, Wireless, Monitor.
Response
Sensors respond at the speeds on your quick reference guide: 15 seconds for oxygen and VOC, 25 for CO, 30 for H2S and LEL, and 200 for HCN. Give a reading time to settle and to clear.
Test gases
The HCN cylinder is balanced in nitrogen, so the oxygen reading falls and alarms while it flows. The 4-gas mix holds 18% oxygen, which is below the 19.5% low alarm.
Vapor
In a custom atmosphere, pick the VOC vapor or combustible gas and set its true concentration with the slider or by typing. The monitor shows what its sensor would report: the true amount divided by that gas's correction factor until Meas. Gas is set to the same gas, then 1:1.
Reverse direction
Hold N/− for 5 seconds on a reading screen, menu or list. The arrow on the display flips from right to left or from down to up, and N/− then steps backward. Hold it 5 seconds again to switch back.
Time multiplier
Speeds up startup, sensor warm-up, calibration countdowns, how fast readings rise and fall, and the exposure clock, so the monitor is ready and calibrated sooner, readings settle sooner, and TWA, STEL, Average and the datalog count build faster. Key holds stay in real time. At 1× fresh air calibration takes 60 seconds, span 60 seconds, and HCN span 230 seconds.
Cross-sensitivity
Sensors also respond to gases other than their own. HCN reads about 6 ppm for every 1 ppm of H2S and 5% of the CO level. CO reads about 20% of the H2S level, and H2S about 1% of the CO level. The VOC sensor sees H2S at one third of its concentration, and it responds to flammable vapors that a 10.6 eV lamp can ionize: the combustible gas you pick also raises the VOC reading by its ppm divided by its PID correction factor. Methane, propane and hydrogen give no PID response. LEL picks up a VOC vapor when flammability data exists for it, plus CO and HCN at small values, and reads low when oxygen is under 10%. With the 4-gas mix flowing, expect HCN to go over range and VOC to show about 3 ppm. Untick the box under "Gas at the inlet" to switch this off.
Calibrate
Pick the gas under "Gas at the inlet" first. Fresh Air needs clean air. Multi Sensor Span uses the 4-gas mix for oxygen, LEL, CO and H2S. HCN and VOC are spanned one at a time from Single Sensor Span with their own cylinders. Single Sensor Span shows the span value and "Apply Gas"; the countdown starts by itself about 2 seconds after the sensor sees the gas, or when you press Start. Each ends with Pass or Fail per sensor. If a fresh air or zero calibration is started with gas still at the inlet, or a bump or span calibration without the right gas applied (or with a span value or calibration gas setting that does not match), the trainer pauses it and tells you what to press or change; you can also proceed anyway or cancel. The span math removes the share of each reading that comes from the other gases in a mixed cylinder, so the 4-gas mix gives an exact calibration.
Bump test
The manual method: apply gas on the reading screen and confirm every tested sensor reaches at least its low alarm. The menu and the Y/+ with N/− 5-second shortcut are from later firmware; a sensor that fails shows "Bump" until it passes.
Over range
When a gas exceeds a sensor's range (VOC above 1,000 ppm, for example) the sensor shows "Over" and its reading flashes 9999, as the user guide describes. It is a warning, not a measurement.
Alarms
High, Over and pump: 3 beeps a second. Low: 2. STEL, TWA, Neg and Cal: 1. Limits: CO 35/200 ppm, oxygen 19.5/23.5%, HCN 4.7/25 ppm, H2S 10/20 ppm, LEL 10/20%, VOC 50/100 ppm. In Search mode the beep rate rises from 1 to 7 a second with concentration.
LEL gases
Change Meas. Gas for LEL lists 97 gases with the MultiRAE correction factors from Technical Note TN-156. The monitor applies the measurement gas factor divided by the calibration gas factor.
Man Down
Turn it on under Alarms, then tick "lying motionless". After the motionless time the monitor asks "Are You OK?". Y/+ cancels, N/− or no answer raises the alarm.

Screens, menus and the alarm table follow the MultiRAE User's Guide (Rev C). Low and high alarm limits, resolution, range and response time are from your agency's quick reference guide (MultiRAE Lite section); calibration gases from TN-114; LEL factors from TN-156; VOC factors from TN-106B. STEL and TWA limits and most default settings are not in those documents and are typical values to confirm against your monitors. Cross-sensitivities are from TN-114 (CO, H2S, HCN), TN-106B (PID) and TN-156 (LEL); where TN-114 gives a range the middle is used.

Events

Key guide
G7c extras
This full version adds features from the manual that your unit does not show. Main menu, PTT channels: Enter channel # (two digits, then Edit/Yes/No), Receive only, All call and Change volume; Quick select also shows the current PTT channel. Modes and Quick select add SCBA (30-minute timeout), Leak check (pumped, 30-minute timeout, block test first) and High risk (no timeout). Audio pairing adds Reconnect and Forget device. With Banner set to Check-in, the top right corner counts down to the next check-in. The G7oc version leaves all of these out. The pump holds close to the data sheet's 300 ml/min target flow, and the green light blinks while the G7 connects.
Sensor under limit
If a CO, H2S, HCN or LEL reading drops well below zero (usually after zeroing in gas), the reading shows UL and a low urgency "Sensor under limit" notification appears. Blackline recommends calibrating the device. The trainer triggers it 10% of the low alarm below zero (for example -3.5 ppm CO), because Blackline does not publish the threshold.
Power
Hold the power button for 2 seconds. Start-up matches a recording of your G7c: one long beep and a red, yellow, blue light sweep, the alarm test result, four feature screens (speakerphone, messaging, GPS, indoor location), the set points for each sensor, about 1 minute 45 seconds of "Please wait while sensors initialize...", next calibration and bump dates, an automatic zero (the LEL-MPS sensor shows "-" because it zeroes itself), then the readings. About 2 minutes 40 seconds in all at 1×; the time multiplier shortens it. The green light comes on about 24 seconds in, once the G7 is connected, and stays on (in the G7c version it blinks while connecting, as the manual and data sheet describe). Without a cellular connection it blinks. To switch off, keep holding: "Hold to shutdown..." counts 3, 2, 1 with a yellow flash and a beep on each count (let go to cancel), then "blacklinesafety logging off..." for about 3 seconds, as on your unit.
OK
Opens the Main menu from the home screen and selects menu items.
▲ and ▼
Scroll menus. From the home screen a single press opens Quick select. On Yes/No screens, ▲ is Yes and ▼ is No; on Edit/Yes/No screens, ▲ is Edit, OK is Yes and ▼ is No.
Mute
Hold ▲ and ▼ together. A notification must be on screen for 2 seconds first. Gas alert lights stay on until the gas clears; a muted high gas alert sounds again after 60 seconds, and a low gas warning after 2 minutes, while the gas is still there.
Latch
Pull it for an SOS alert. Push it to acknowledge a potential fall or no-motion, or to answer a check-in request. If one of those escalates to a red alert, hold the latch in for 2 seconds. Holding the latch in for 3 seconds from the home screen checks in early.
Bump test
Gas options, Bump test, Yes. After the light and sound test, pick the sensors and choose Start bump, then apply gas inside the 60-second window. The 4-gas mix covers CO, H2S, O2 and LEL; clear HCN's checkbox first and the G7 asks for it again afterwards.
Calibration
Gas options, Calibration, Yes. Start zeroing in clean air, then select the gas mix and Start span. The LEL-MPS sensor keeps its factory calibration and shows "-". Once every sensor has seen its gas, the G7 waits for the readings to settle, then shows "Turn gas off" with the live readings; press OK for the result.
Bump test due
A bump test becomes due 24 hours after the last one that passed, or straight away if a bump test fails (sooner with the time multiplier, or use "Make the bump test due"). The status screen stays up: the top bar alternates between "Bump test due" and the normal bar every 2 seconds, and the G7 double-chirps about every 3 seconds. The chirps stop while it is docked. A passed bump test clears it.
G7 Dock
Use "G7 Dock" below. Docking a powered-on G7 brings up the Dock menu after about a second (Bump test, Calibration, Advanced info). The dock's Advanced info has Inlet settings (the gas on each inlet; ▲ ▼ scroll a line, OK goes back), Dock update ("Do not remove / Downloading updates...", then "Update complete") and Dock info (model, dock ID, version). Choose Bump test with OK and close the lid: the G7 starts as soon as the lid is closed. It runs the light and sound test, then "Bump test..." while the dock feeds HCN (the reading climbs slowly and levels off near 5 to 6 ppm, as on your unit), then the 4-gas mix. At the end the title alternates between "Ready to remove" and "Bump test result". Press OK, or take the G7 out ("Out of the dock"): it shows "Next bump test", then "Please wait..." for about 30 s, then the Dock menu (if still docked) or the status screen. About 1 minute 15 seconds of gas at 1×. Choosing Calibration instead runs "Zeroing..." (about 30 s), then "Calibrating..." with HCN for about 5 minutes and the 4-gas mix for about 2 minutes, as on your unit: about a minute into each gas the toxic readings jump to the cylinder value (10.0, 100, 25.0) and hold there. The result alternates "Ready to remove" and "Calibration done". Press OK, or take the G7 out, for "Next calibration" (30 days), then "Please wait..." for about 30 s, then the Dock menu (still docked) or the status screen. The 7-minute calibration is a good place to use the time multiplier. While the G7 is docked the gas buttons above have no effect, because the sensors sample the dock's test chamber. Tick an empty cylinder to see a sensor fail.
Messages
Main menu, Messages, Send a message. Choosing a ready-made message sends it at once ("Sending...", then the status screen). *Create custom*: ▲ ▼ change the character (▲ from blank gives A; the order runs 0-9, A-Z, then . ? ! and a space), OK moves to the next character, and OK twice sends. "Send message?" then offers Edit (▲), Yes (OK) and No (▼, back to the message list). Messages sent to the device with the Events button appear in Message inbox.
Timer
Main menu, Time, Timer. Set the unit first (sec or min with ▲ ▼), then OK for the tens digit and OK for the ones digit, then OK again: "Set timer for ...?" Edit/Yes/No. The countdown shows beside Timer in the Time menu. "Timer done" stays on screen until you hold ▲ and ▼, then the status screen returns.
Time
Stopwatch: OK starts and pauses, ▲ resets once paused, ▼ goes back. Time settings, Custom offset: sign, then hours (00 to 12), then minutes in 15-minute steps, then "Change time offset to ...?" Edit/Yes/No; Yes returns to the status screen.
Accessories
Audio pairing: Status ("No device paired") and Pair new, which scans for about 38 seconds, lists "BT Device", then shows "Connecting... Please wait..." and "Error, Unable to connect", as recorded on your unit.
Settings
Banner switches between Check-in and Time (local time in the top right corner); Yes returns to the status screen. Languages: any language other than English triggers the trainer's safety stop. Pump options: Sample time ON/OFF ("Sample timer enabled/disabled", then the status screen) and Hose length (unit first, then the two digits, then "Change hose length to ...?"; up to 99 ft or 30 m, the data sheet's maximum).
Advanced info
Device info, User info, GPS location, Beacons and Comm info. Device info uses trainer values, not your unit's ID or activation code.
Gas info
Main menu, Gas options, View gas info: Gas set points, Last completed, Next due dates, Peak readings, Reset peaks, Calibration gas, STEL calculation, TWA calculation, Reset TWA/STEL and LEL latching. OK moves through multi-page screens; ▲ ▼ scroll lists longer than five lines (Peak readings). Peaks are tracked all the time. Gas notifications are non-latching: they clear by themselves once the gas is back to normal. With LEL latching ON (manual section 7.6.4), an LEL high gas alert stays in high urgency after the gas clears until you hold ▲ and ▼ to mute it; with it OFF, it clears by itself like the others. Going back to any menu highlights its top item, as on your unit.
Modes
From the home screen press ▼ or ▲ for Quick select, which shows pump status (on or off, hose length, flow), "Pre-entry mode" and "Pump run mode". OK on a mode that is OFF starts it straight away with a block test, as on your G7c: "Starting pump" (do not block, about 15 seconds until the flow settles), then "Testing flow / Block inlet" (tick "Block the pump inlet" below until it changes), then "Unblock inlet" (untick it). Each step has 60 seconds; if one runs out the G7 shows "Block test failed, Check tubing & try again" and goes back to normal. In a mode the top bar alternates between "Mode:" and the pump icon; Pre-entry also counts down 5 minutes and then asks whether to continue. OK on a mode that is ON stops it. Holding ▲ shows pump details and holding ▼ leaves the mode.
Time multiplier
Speeds up start-up, sensor warm-up, how fast readings rise and fall, the 60-second bump and calibration windows, the zero, TWA and STEL, the 60-minute check-in timer, the 5-minute no-motion timer and the 5-minute Pre-entry timeout. The 30-second response windows for falls, no-motion and check-ins, the block test and key holds stay in real time.
Cross-sensitivity
From Blackline's Sensor Cross-Sensitivities sheet, for your cartridge's combined CO/H2S sensor (North America), HCN and LEL-MPS sensors. Pick one from "Interfering gas or vapor at the sensors" below the gas buttons: hydrogen, nitric oxide, nitrogen dioxide, sulfur dioxide, carbon dioxide, isopropanol, ethanol or methanol. The note under it shows what each sensor should read.
CO sensor: H2S reads about 4% (15 ppm H2S shows 0.6 ppm CO), H2 about 20% (100 ppm H2 shows 20 ppm CO).
H2S sensor: CO reads about 2% (300 ppm CO shows under 6 ppm H2S), SO2 about 20% (5 ppm shows under 1 ppm).
Alcohol vapors (de-icing fluid, sanitizer, washer fluid and the like) can poison the CO/H2S sensor.
HCN sensor: NO reads about 5%, NO2 about 70%, and H2 only after about 5 minutes, once its filter is saturated (then about 40%).
LEL-MPS sensor: does not detect H2S; oxygen above 21.8% reads 9.7% LEL; CO2 above 5000 ppm reads 1.75% LEL per 1000 ppm, so breathing on the sensor can give a false LEL reading; below 10% oxygen (the HCN cylinder is balanced in nitrogen) it goes into a sensor error that only a restart clears.
LEL-MPS gases: the sensor gives true %LEL, without correction factors, for hydrogen, methane, ethane, propane, butane, isobutane, isopropanol, pentane, hexane, octane, toluene, xylene, propylene, isobutylene and methyl ethyl ketone (ethylene in Blackline's earlier lists). It sorts what it sees into six classes (1 hydrogen; 2 hydrogen and hydrocarbon mix; 3 methane or natural gas; 4 light: ethane, propane, butane, isopropanol; 5 medium: pentane, hexane; 6 heavy: octane, toluene, xylene), which are logged in Blackline Live, not shown on the G7. Hydrogen picked as the interfering gas also reads on the LEL sensor (40,000 ppm is 100% LEL).
The 4-gas mix therefore shows about 101 ppm CO and 27 ppm H2S. Untick "Sensors react to cross-sensitive gases" to switch all of this off.

Screens, menus, prompts and procedures follow the G7 Technical User Manual (Rev. 33) and Blackline's help centre. Set points, the start-up screens and their timing come from a recording of your own G7c. Response times, the zero duration, the check-in, no-motion and mode timeout values, and the bump pass level are not in those documents and are typical values to confirm against your Blackline Live configuration profile.
